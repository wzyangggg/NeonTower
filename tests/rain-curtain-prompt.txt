Built-in image_gen, transparent_background=true.

Use case: game VFX texture. One seamless tileable SQUARE alpha rain texture, straight vertical orientation for UV-scrolling shader production. DENSE HEAVY DOWNPOUR, MANY closely spaced long and medium rain streaks covering the whole tile evenly, overlapping thin streaks and a few broader defocused streaks, variable translucency, grayscale white and silver only. ALL streaks perfectly VERTICAL, zero diagonal slant, zero perspective, zero wind baked into the image. Soft out-of-focus feathered edges and vertical motion blur, about 45 to 60 percent of image occupied by streak alpha, remaining gaps fully transparent, strong density like a curtain of heavy rain, not a sparse drizzle, not an opaque white fog sheet. Uniform density across all four edges, seamless repeat, no border or directional lighting. True transparent RGBA background, no black/gray rectangle. No environment, no ground, no splashes, no lettering, no stars, no colored light. Designed to be tinted, layered, UV-scrolled and optionally slanted entirely in shader.
